### ASQ Bloom's Taxonomy Cognitive Levels (Maximum Tested Depth)
1. **Remember:** Recall or recognize terms, definitions, facts, or basic history.
2. **Understand:** Comprehend concepts, interpret charts, compare philosophies (e.g., Lean vs. Six Sigma).
3. **Apply:** Use formulas, statistical tables, or structured tools in concrete calculations/scenarios.
4. **Analyze:** Break down complex data/scenarios, identify patterns, determine root causes, interpret statistical outputs.
5. **Evaluate:** Judge value/validity, choose optimal statistical tests or strategies, make data-driven business decisions.
6. **Create:** Synthesize elements into an integrated plan or design framework.

## Detailed ASQ CSSBB Body of Knowledge (Sections I–IX)

### I. Organization-Wide Planning and Deployment (12 Questions)
*DMAIC Tag: **Enterprise / Pre-Define (Domain I)***

#### I.A. Organization-Wide Considerations
- **I.A.1. Fundamentals of Six Sigma and lean methodologies** `[Understand]`
  - Define and describe the value, foundations, philosophy, history (Deming, Juran, Crosby, Ishikawa, Taguchi, Ohno, Shingo, Motorola/Bill Smith), and goals of Six Sigma and Lean.
  - Describe the integration and complementary relationship between Six Sigma (reducing variation) and Lean (eliminating waste / improving flow).
  - Identify and understand an organization's Lean Six Sigma maturity model.
- **I.A.2. Six Sigma, lean, and continuous improvement methodologies** `[Apply]`
  - Describe when to use Six Sigma (DMAIC) instead of other problem-solving approaches (e.g., Lean/Kaizen, PDCA, A3, 8D, DFSS, just-do-it).
  - Align Six Sigma objectives with organizational goals; apply project screening and selection criteria.
- **I.A.3. Relationships among business systems and processes** `[Understand]`
  - Describe interactive relationships among business systems, processes, and internal/external stakeholders, and how changes in one subsystem ripple across others.
- **I.A.4. Strategic planning and deployment for initiatives** `[Apply]`
  - Align project selection with strategic business planning (SWOT, PESTLE, Porter's Five Forces, business plans, risk management). *(Note: Hoshin Kanri / X-matrix was officially removed in the 2022 ASQ CSSBB BoK revision.)*
  - Evaluate project portfolios using tangible financial metrics (NPV, ROI, IRR, payback period, CBA) and intangible benefits.

#### I.B. Leadership
- **I.B.1. Roles and responsibilities** `[Evaluate]`
  - Identify, describe, and evaluate roles/responsibilities of Executives, Deployment Champions, Project Sponsors, Master Black Belts (MBBs), Black Belts (BBs), Green Belts (GBs), Yellow Belts, Process Owners, and Finance representatives.
- **I.B.2. Organizational barriers** `[Understand]`
  - Describe how organizational structure (functional, matrix, projectized) and culture impact Six Sigma projects.
  - Identify common causes of Six Sigma deployment failure (lack of executive support, poor project selection, scope creep, lack of resources, disconnected metrics).
- **I.B.3. Change management** `[Apply]`
  - Apply change management techniques (Kotter's 8 steps, Lewin's Unfreeze-Change-Refreeze, Bridges' transition model, force-field analysis), stakeholder analysis, readiness assessments, overcoming resistance, communication plans, and tracking cultural shifts.

### II. Organizational Process Management and Measures (12 Questions)
*DMAIC Tag: **Enterprise / Pre-Define Metrics (Domain II)***

#### II.A. Impact on Stakeholders `[Understand]`
- Describe the impact Six Sigma projects have on customers, suppliers, employees, shareholders, and regulatory stakeholders.

#### II.B. Benchmarking `[Apply]`
- Define and distinguish types of benchmarking: **internal, competitive, functional/collaborative, best-practices, process, strategic, and breakthrough**.
- Select measures and performance goals resulting from benchmarking studies.

#### II.C. Business Measures
- **II.C.1. Performance measures** `[Apply]`
  - Define, select, and apply **Balanced Scorecard** (Financial, Customer, Internal Business Processes, Learning & Growth), **KPIs** (Key Performance Indicators), **KBIs** (Key Behavior Indicators), **OKRs** (Objectives and Key Results), leading vs. lagging indicators, and operational metrics (DPU, DPMO, RTY, FTY, COPQ).
- **II.C.2. Financial measures** `[Apply]`
  - Define, calculate, and analyze **Return on Investment (ROI)**, **Net Present Value (NPV)**, **Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**, **Payback Period**, **Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA)**, **Return on Assets (ROA)**, and **Cost of Quality (COQ / COPQ: Prevention, Appraisal, Internal Failure, External Failure — PAF model)**.

### III. Team Management (15 Questions)
*DMAIC Tag: **Cross-DMAIC Team Leadership (Domain III)***

#### III.A. Team Formation
- **III.A.1. Team types and constraints** `[Apply]`
  - Define and select virtual, cross-functional, self-directed, and ad-hoc/kaizen teams based on geography, technology, schedules, and time zones.
- **III.A.2. Team roles and responsibilities** `[Apply]`
  - Define and apply team roles: leader, facilitator, coach, sponsor/champion, member, scribe/recorder, timekeeper.
- **III.A.3. Team member selection criteria** `[Apply]`
  - Select team members based on subject-matter expertise, authority, credibility, diversity of perspective, and interpersonal skills.

#### III.B. Team Facilitation `[Apply]`
- Apply techniques for team motivation (Herzberg, Maslow, McGregor Theory X/Y), recognition and rewards.
- Address **Lencioni's Five Dysfunctions of a Team** (absence of trust, fear of conflict, lack of commitment, avoidance of accountability, inattention to results).
- Manage **Tuckman's stages of team development**: **Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing, Adjourning**.
- Facilitate effective agendas, ground rules, decision-making (consensus, multivoting, Nominal Group Technique [NGT]), and internal/external communication.

#### III.C. Team Dynamics `[Apply]`
- Identify and manage group behaviors (groupthink, Abilene paradox, halo effect) and individual disruptive behaviors (dominating, withdrawn/reluctant, side conversations, digressing, feuding).
- Apply conflict resolution modes (**Thomas-Kilmann**: Competing/Forcing, Collaborating, Compromising, Avoiding, Accommodating).

#### III.D. Team Training `[Apply]`
- Conduct training needs assessments, adult learning design (ADDIE / Kirkpatrick's 4 levels of training evaluation), select/develop materials, coach Green Belts, and evaluate training effectiveness.

### IV. Define Phase (20 Questions)
*DMAIC Tag: **DEFINE Phase (Domain IV)***

#### IV.A. Voice of the Customer (VOC)
- **IV.A.1. Customer identification** `[Apply]`
  - Identify and segment internal and external customers; map project impact on customer segments.
- **IV.A.2. Customer data collection** `[Analyze]`
  - Select and analyze VOC collection methods (surveys, focus groups, interviews, gemba observations, complaint logs, warranty data).
  - Evaluate VOC instruments for **validity** (measuring the right construct) and **reliability** (consistency/repeatability), avoiding sampling/question bias.
- **IV.A.3. Customer requirements** `[Evaluate]`
  - Translate vague VOC into measurable **Critical-to-X (CTX / CTQ, CTC, CTD, CTS)** trees, **Kano Model** (Must-be/Basic, One-dimensional/Performance, Attractive/Delighters, Indifferent, Reverse), and **Quality Function Deployment (QFD / House of Quality)**.

#### IV.B. Business Case and Project Charter
- **IV.B.1. Business case** `[Understand]`
  - Describe financial and non-financial justification and strategic alignment.
- **IV.B.2. Problem statement** `[Evaluate]`
  - Define and evaluate problem statements: MUST state *what* is wrong, *where/when*, *baseline metric & magnitude/pain*, and MUST exclude *blame, root causes, and proposed solutions*.
- **IV.B.3. Project scope** `[Analyze]`
  - Develop and review project boundaries (in-scope vs. out-of-scope, **SIPOC** high-level boundary, longitudinal/lateral scoping) to prevent scope creep while delivering customer value.
- **IV.B.4. Goals and objectives** `[Analyze]`
  - Formulate **SMART** (Specific, Measurable, Achievable/Actionable, Relevant, Time-bound) goals linked directly to the problem statement baseline.
- **IV.B.5. Project performance measurements** `[Analyze]`
  - Select primary metrics (e.g., cycle time, defect rate) and consequential/guardrail metrics (ensuring improving one metric doesn't degrade safety/quality/cost).
- **IV.B.6. Project charter review** `[Understand]`
  - Explain the importance of living charter reviews with sponsors/stakeholders as process knowledge evolves.

#### IV.C. Project Management (PM) Tools `[Evaluate]`
- Select, construct, and evaluate:
  - **Gantt charts**
  - **Tollgate (phase-gate) reviews**
  - **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)**
  - **RACI matrix** (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed)
  - **Critical Path Method (CPM) / PERT** (early/late start & finish, slack/float, crashing)

#### IV.D. Analytical Tools (7 Management & Planning Tools) `[Apply]`
- Identify and apply across DMAIC (tested under Section IV in ASQ BoK):
  - **Affinity diagrams** (organizing large volumes of verbal ideas/VOC into natural themes)
  - **Interrelationship digraphs (ID)** (mapping cause-and-effect arrows among complex interrelated issues; most outgoing arrows = root driver, most incoming = key outcome)
  - **Tree diagrams** (breaking broad goals/CTQs into hierarchical sub-levels)
  - **Matrix diagrams** (L, T, Y, X, C-shaped matrices showing strength of relationships)
  - **Prioritization matrices** (weighted criteria decision matrices)
  - **Process Decision Program Charts (PDPC)** (contingency planning / "what could go wrong" on a tree plan)
  - **Activity Network Diagrams (AND / AON / AOA)** (sequencing parallel/serial project tasks)

### V. Measure Phase (25 Questions — Highest Exam Weight!)
*DMAIC Tag: **MEASURE Phase (Domain V)***

#### V.A. Process Characteristics
- **V.A.1. Process flow metrics** `[Analyze]`
  - Calculate and analyze **WIP** (Work in Process), **WIQ** (Work in Queue), **Touch Time / Value-Added Time**, **Cycle Time**, **Lead Time** (**Little's Law:** $\text{Lead Time} = \frac{\text{WIP}}{\text{Throughput Rate}}$), **Takt Time** ($\frac{\text{Available Production Time}}{\text{Customer Demand}}$), **Throughput**, **FTY** (First Time Yield), and **RTY** (Rolled Throughput Yield = $Y_1 \times Y_2 \times \dots \times Y_k$ or $e^{-\text{DPU}}$).
  - Analyze the impact of **"hidden factories"** (rework loops invisible to final yield).
- **V.A.2. Process analysis tools** `[Evaluate]`
  - Select, construct, and evaluate **SIPOC**, detailed **Process Maps** (cross-functional / swimlane, deployment flowcharts), **Value Stream Maps (VSM)** (current state, % value-added), **Spaghetti diagrams**, **Circle diagrams**, and **Gemba walks**. *(ASQ Exam Note: Detailed process mapping & VSM analysis are tested primarily in Measure V.A.2!)*

#### V.B. Data Collection
- **V.B.1. Types of data and measurement scales** `[Apply]`
  - Distinguish **qualitative vs. quantitative** and **continuous (variables) vs. discrete (attribute: count/proportion)** data.
  - Apply **Nominal, Ordinal, Interval, and Ratio (NOIR)** measurement scales and select valid statistical methods for each.
- **V.B.2. Sampling** `[Apply]`
  - Apply random, simple random, stratified, systematic, cluster, and rational subgroup sampling; ensure sample homogeneity/representativeness.
- **V.B.3. Data collection plans and methods** `[Apply]`
  - Develop operational definitions, check sheets, concentration diagrams, data coding/standardization, and automated data capture; prevent common data errors (bias, rounding, truncation, transcription errors) and perform data cleaning.

#### V.C. Measurement Systems
- **V.C.1. Measurement methods** `[Understand]`
  - Describe metrology fundamentals, calibration traceability (NIST/ISO), continuous gages vs. attribute/go-no-go inspection.
- **V.C.2. Measurement Systems Analysis (MSA)** `[Evaluate]`
  - Distinguish **Accuracy** (**Bias**, **Linearity**, **Stability**) vs. **Precision** (**Repeatability** = within-operator/equipment variation $EV$; **Reproducibility** = between-operator variation $AV$).
  - Design, calculate, and evaluate **Gage R&R** (Crossed vs. Nested; $\bar{X}-R$ and **ANOVA** methods with operator-by-part interaction), **%StudyVar**, **%Tolerance (P/T ratio)**, **%Contribution (variance)**, and **Number of Distinct Categories ($ndc \ge 5$)**.
    - AIAG thresholds for %StudyVar or P/T: **< 10%** acceptable; **10%–30%** marginal (depends on cost/risk); **> 30%** unacceptable. (%Contribution variance thresholds are **< 1%**, **1%–9%**, **> 9%**!).
  - Evaluate **Attribute Agreement Analysis / Audit MSA** (within-appraiser, between-appraiser, appraiser vs. standard, **Cohen's / Fleiss' Kappa** $\kappa > 0.75$ good, $> 0.90$ excellent).

#### V.D. Basic Statistics
- **V.D.1. Basic statistical terms** `[Understand]`
  - Population parameters ($\mu, \sigma, p$) vs. sample statistics ($\bar{x}, s, \hat{p}$); degrees of freedom.
- **V.D.2. Central Limit Theorem (CLT)** `[Understand]`
  - Sampling distribution of the sample mean $\bar{X} \sim N(\mu, \sigma/\sqrt{n})$; role in confidence intervals, control charts, and hypothesis testing.
- **V.D.3. Descriptive statistics** `[Apply]`
  - Calculate and interpret mean, median, mode, trimmed mean, range, IQR, variance ($s^2$), standard deviation ($s$), skewness, kurtosis, quartiles/percentiles.
- **V.D.4. Graphical methods** `[Analyze]`
  - Construct and interpret histograms, box-and-whisker plots, stem-and-leaf plots, scatter diagrams, run charts, Pareto charts, normal probability plots, and **Multi-Vari charts** (positional, cyclical, temporal variation).
- **V.D.5. Valid statistical conclusions** `[Understand]`
  - Distinguish **enumerative** (descriptive of finite frame) vs. **analytic** (inferential prediction on an ongoing process — Deming).

#### V.E. Probability
- **V.E.1. Basic concepts** `[Apply]`
  - Independence, mutually exclusive events, addition rule $P(A \cup B) = P(A)+P(B)-P(A \cap B)$, multiplication rule, conditional probability $P(A|B)$, Bayes' theorem, permutations & combinations.
- **V.E.2. Distributions** `[Apply]`
  - Select, calculate probabilities, and apply:
    - **Discrete:** Binomial, Poisson, Hypergeometric.
    - **Continuous:** Normal ($Z$), Lognormal, Exponential, Weibull, Bivariate Normal, and sampling distributions (**Student's $t$, Chi-Square $\chi^2$, $F$-distribution**).

#### V.F. Process Capability
- **V.F.1. Process capability studies** `[Evaluate]`
  - Verify prerequisites (**statistical stability/control first**, **normality**, **valid MSA**); design capability studies.
- **V.F.2. Process performance vs. specification** `[Evaluate]`
  - Distinguish natural **control limits** ($\mu \pm 3\sigma$, voice of the process) from **specification limits** ($\text{LSL}, \text{USL}$, voice of the customer) and Taguchi target value.
- **V.F.3. Process capability indices** `[Evaluate]`
  - Calculate and interpret **$C_p, C_{pk}, C_{pm}$** (short-term / within-subgroup $\hat{\sigma}_{within} = \bar{R}/d_2$ or $\bar{s}/c_4$) and **$C_r$** ($1/C_p$).
- **V.F.4. Process performance indices** `[Evaluate]`
  - Calculate and interpret **$P_p, P_{pk}$** (long-term / overall sample standard deviation $s_{overall}$).
  - Understand when $C_p = C_{pk}$ (process centered) and when $C_{pk} \approx P_{pk}$ (minimal between-subgroup drift).
- **V.F.5. Short-term vs. long-term capability & non-normal / attribute capability** `[Evaluate]`
  - Understand the **1.5$\sigma$ shift** convention ($Z_{ST} = Z_{LT} + 1.5$; e.g., $6\sigma_{ST} \leftrightarrow 4.5\sigma_{LT} \leftrightarrow 3.4\text{ DPMO}$).
  - Handle **non-normal data** (Box-Cox transformation, Johnson transformation, Weibull/percentile method $C_{npk}$).
  - Calculate **attribute capability** (DPU, DPMO, ppm, Sigma level).

### VI. Analyze Phase (22 Questions — 2nd Highest Exam Weight!)
*DMAIC Tag: **ANALYZE Phase (Domain VI)***

#### VI.A. Measuring and Modeling Relationships Between Variables
- **VI.A.1. Correlation coefficient** `[Evaluate]`
  - Calculate, test significance ($t = r\sqrt{\frac{n-2}{1-r^2}}$), and interpret Pearson's $r$ ($-1 \le r \le +1$) and coefficient of determination $R^2$; remember **correlation $\ne$ causation** and check scatter plots for curvature/outliers.
- **VI.A.2. Linear regression** `[Evaluate]`
  - Calculate simple linear regression ($\hat{y} = b_0 + b_1 x$) via least squares; interpret **multiple linear regression** outputs ($R^2$, $R^2_{\text{adj}}$, ANOVA $F$-test, predictor $p$-values, **VIF / multicollinearity**).
  - Perform **residual analysis** (normality, constant variance / homoscedasticity, independence / Durbin-Watson, outliers / leverage).
  - Use regression models for prediction intervals vs. confidence intervals; understand logistic / non-linear regression basics.
- **VI.A.3. Multivariate tools** `[Understand]` *(Cognitive level note: Understand!)*
  - Identify when to use MANOVA, Principal Components Analysis (PCA), Factor Analysis, Discriminant Analysis, and Cluster Analysis.

#### VI.B. Hypothesis Testing
- **VI.B.1. Terminology & basics** `[Evaluate]`
  - Formulate $H_0$ and $H_a$ (one-tailed vs. two-tailed); interpret **Type I error ($\alpha$, producer's risk / false alarm)**, **Type II error ($\beta$, consumer's risk / miss)**, **Power ($1-\beta$)**, **$p$-value**, **statistical vs. practical significance**, and **sample size** calculations ($n$ for estimating means/proportions with margin of error $E$).
- **VI.B.2. Point and interval estimation** `[Apply]`
  - Calculate and interpret **confidence intervals** for $\mu$ ($Z$ vs. $t$), $\sigma^2$ ($\chi^2$), and $p$; understand confidence intervals vs. prediction intervals vs. **statistical tolerance intervals**.
- **VI.B.3. Tests for means, variances, and proportions** `[Evaluate]`
  - Select, calculate, and interpret:
    - **1-sample & 2-sample $Z$-test and $t$-test** (pooled equal variance vs. unpooled Welch's $t$-test)
    - **Paired-comparison $t$-test** (dependent samples $d_i = x_{1i} - x_{2i}$)
    - **Variance tests:** 1-variance $\chi^2$ test; 2-variance **$F$-test**; 3+ variances (**Bartlett's test** for normal data, **Levene's test** for non-normal data)
    - **1-proportion & 2-proportion $Z$-tests**
    - **One-way and Two-way ANOVA** (sum of squares $SS_{\text{Total}} = SS_{\text{Treatment}} + SS_{\text{Error}}$, degrees of freedom, $MS$, $F$-ratio, post-hoc Tukey/Fisher LSD)
    - **Goodness-of-fit and Contingency Table Tests** ($\chi^2$ test for independence/homogeneity; expected cell count $\ge 5$).
- **VI.B.4. Non-parametric tests** `[Evaluate]`
  - Select and interpret distribution-free alternatives (test medians / ranks when data are skewed, ordinal, or heavily non-normal):
    - 1-sample: **1-Sample Sign test**, **1-Sample Wilcoxon Signed-Rank test**
    - 2 independent samples: **Mann-Whitney $U$ test** (counterpart to 2-sample $t$-test)
    - 2 paired samples: **Paired Wilcoxon Signed-Rank test**
    - 3+ independent samples: **Kruskal-Wallis test** (counterpart to One-way ANOVA)
    - 3+ blocked/related treatments: **Friedman test** (counterpart to Randomized Block ANOVA)
    - Randomness: **Runs test**; Variance: **Mood's median test**.

#### VI.C. Risk Analysis and Management *(Expanded in 2022 BoK!)*
- **VI.C.1. Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA)** `[Evaluate]`
  - Distinguish **DFMEA** (Design) vs. **PFMEA** (Process).
  - Evaluate **Severity ($S$), Occurrence ($O$), Detection ($D$)**, **RPN = $S \times O \times D$**, and Action Priority (AP); understand why **high Severity ($S = 9, 10$) always requires attention regardless of RPN**, and why reducing $S$ requires design/process change while poka-yoke reduces $O$ or improves $D$. *(ASQ Exam Note: ASQ places FMEA & Risk Management in Section VI Analyze, though FMEA is updated in Improve/Control!)*
- **VI.C.2. Risk management** `[Evaluate]`
  - Apply ISO 31000 / enterprise risk identification, qualitative/quantitative risk assessment, risk matrices, mitigation strategies (avoid, transfer, mitigate, accept), and ongoing risk monitoring.

#### VI.D. Additional Analysis Methods
- **VI.D.1. Gap analysis** `[Analyze]`
  - Compare current baseline performance against target/benchmark to isolate performance gaps.
- **VI.D.2. Root cause analysis (RCA)** `[Evaluate]`
  - Apply and evaluate **Ishikawa / Fishbone (Cause-and-Effect) diagrams** (6Ms: Man/People, Machine, Material, Method, Measurement, Mother Nature/Environment), **5 Whys**, **Fault Tree Analysis (FTA: AND / OR logic gates)**, **Cause & Effect (X-Y) Matrix**, and **Pareto analysis** (80/20 vital few).
- **VI.D.3. Waste analysis** `[Analyze]`
  - Identify the **7+1 Muda / Wastes (TIMWOODS / DOWNTIME)**: Defects, Overproduction, Waiting, Non-utilized talent, Transportation, Inventory, Motion, Extra-processing — plus **Mura** (unevenness) and **Muri** (overburden).

### VII. Improve Phase (21 Questions — 3rd Highest Exam Weight!)
*DMAIC Tag: **IMPROVE Phase (Domain VII)***

#### VII.A. Design of Experiments (DOE)
- **VII.A.1. Terminology** `[Understand]`
  - Independent variables (**factors**), **levels** (low $-1$ / high $+1$), dependent variable (**response**), **treatment / run**, **error / residual**, **repetition vs. replication** (true replication resets setups independently to estimate pure experimental error!), **nuisance/noise factors**, **covariates (ANCOVA)**.
- **VII.A.2. Design principles** `[Apply]`
  - Apply **Power & Sample Size**, **Balance**, **Orthogonality** (independent factor estimates), **Randomization** (protects against lurking/time-order bias), **Blocking** (isolates known nuisance variation like batches/shifts), **Confounding / Aliasing**, and **Resolution (III, IV, V)**.
    - **Resolution III:** Main effects clear of each other, but confounded with 2-way interactions ($I = ABC$). Good for screening only.
    - **Resolution IV:** Main effects clear of 2-way interactions, but 2-way interactions confounded with each other ($I = ABCD$).
    - **Resolution V:** Main effects AND 2-way interactions clear of each other ($I = ABCDE$). Needed to model interactions!
- **VII.A.3. Planning and organizing experiments** `[Evaluate]`
  - Select factors, levels, ranges, center points (detect curvature & estimate pure error without replicating all corners!), and sequential experimentation strategy.
- **VII.A.4. Design types (CRD, RBD, Latin Square)** `[Apply]`
  - Select and analyze Completely Randomized Designs (CRD), Randomized Block Designs (RBD — 1 blocking factor), and Latin Square Designs (2 blocking factors, no interactions).
- **VII.A.5. Full factorial experiments ($2^k$ and general)** `[Evaluate]`
  - Design $2^k$ factorials in standard (Yates) order; **calculate main effects and interaction effects** ($\text{Effect} = \bar{Y}_{+} - \bar{Y}_{-}$; regression coefficient $= \text{Effect}/2$); interpret **Main Effects Plots, Interaction Plots (non-parallel lines = interaction!), Normal/Half-Normal Pareto plots of effects**, and ANOVA tables.
  - Note: When a 2-way interaction $AB$ is statistically significant, interpret the $AB$ interaction plot together rather than interpreting main effects $A$ or $B$ in isolation!
- **VII.A.6. Two-level fractional factorial experiments ($2^{k-p}$)** `[Evaluate]`
  - Construct and analyze half/quarter fractions, defining relations ($I$), alias structures, fold-over designs; understand **Response Surface Methodology (RSM: Central Composite Design [CCD], Box-Behnken, steepest ascent)** and **EVOP** (Evolutionary Operation) concepts.

#### VII.B. Lean Methods
- **VII.B.1. Waste elimination** `[Apply]`
  - Apply **Pull systems**, **Kanban** (calculate number of kanban cards $N = \frac{D \times T \times (1 + S)}{C}$), **5S** (Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize, Sustain), **Standard Work**, and **Poka-Yoke** (mistake-proofing: shutdown, control, warning).
- **VII.B.2. Cycle-time reduction** `[Evaluate]`
  - Apply **Continuous / One-Piece Flow**, **Cellular manufacturing**, **SMED** (Single-Minute Exchange of Die: separate internal vs. external setup -> convert internal to external -> streamline both), **Line Balancing**, and **Heijunka** (level scheduling of volume and mix).
- **VII.B.3. Kaizen and kaizen blitz** `[Apply]`
  - Distinguish ongoing incremental Kaizen vs. focused 3–5 day Kaizen Blitz events.
- **VII.B.4. Theory of Constraints (TOC) & OEE** `[Apply]`
  - Apply Goldratt's **5 Focusing Steps of TOC** (Identify constraint -> Exploit -> Subordinate -> Elevate -> Repeat; Drum-Buffer-Rope).
  - Calculate and evaluate **Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)**:
    $$\text{OEE} = \text{Availability} \times \text{Performance} \times \text{Quality}$$

#### VII.C. Implementation `[Evaluate]`
- Generate creative solutions (brainstorming, TRIZ, benchmarking), select optimal solutions (Pugh matrix, weighted prioritization matrix, CBA), run **pilot tests** and **simulations** (Monte Carlo / discrete-event), manage implementation plans, and verify post-improvement statistical improvement & updated FMEA.

### VIII. Control Phase (17 Questions)
*DMAIC Tag: **CONTROL Phase (Domain VIII)***

#### VIII.A. Statistical Process Control (SPC)
- **VIII.A.1. Objectives** `[Understand]`
  - Distinguish **common cause (random/noise)** vs. **special/assignable cause** variation; avoid **tampering / over-adjustment** (Deming funnel experiment).
- **VIII.A.2. Selection of variables & rational subgrouping** `[Apply]`
  - Select critical input/process variables ($X$s) vs. output ($Y$s); apply **rational subgrouping** (maximize *between*-subgroup special-cause sensitivity while minimizing *within*-subgroup variation to pure common cause).
- **VIII.A.3. Control chart selection and calculation** `[Evaluate]`
  - **Variables Data (Continuous):**
    - $n = 1$ (individual observations, destructive/slow/batch): **$I\text{-}MR$ (Individuals & Moving Range)**
    - $2 \le n \le 9$ (typical $n = 4, 5$): **$\bar{X}\text{-}R$ chart** ($\text{UCL}_{\bar{X}} = \bar{\bar{X}} + A_2\bar{R}$, $\text{UCL}_R = D_4\bar{R}$)
    - $n \ge 10$ or variable $n$: **$\bar{X}\text{-}s$ chart**
  - **Attributes Data (Discrete):**
    - Defectives (pass/fail items), variable or constant $n$: **$p$-chart** (proportion defective)
    - Defectives, constant $n$ only: **$np$-chart** (count of defectives)
    - Defects (flaws per unit), constant inspection area/sample: **$c$-chart** (Poisson count)
    - Defects per unit, variable sample size/area: **$u$-chart**
  - **Specialized Charts (Small Shifts / Short Runs):**
    - **CUSUM** (Cumulative Sum) & **EWMA** (Exponentially Weighted Moving Average) — detect small sustained shifts ($0.5\sigma$–$1.5\sigma$) faster than Shewhart charts!
    - **Short-Run SPC** (Target/Nominal Z-charts / DNOM for high-mix low-volume production) and **Pre-Control** (stoplight green/yellow/red zones based on spec limits when $C_{pk}$ is high).
- **VIII.A.4. Control chart analysis** `[Evaluate]`
  - Interpret **Western Electric / Nelson out-of-control rules** (1 point beyond $3\sigma$, 2 of 3 beyond $2\sigma$ same side, 4 of 5 beyond $1\sigma$ same side, 8/9 consecutive points on one side of centerline, 6 points trending up/down, 14 points alternating, 15 points hugging centerline within $1\sigma$ = stratification/improper subgrouping!).
  - Note: Always check the **$R$ or $s$ dispersion chart FIRST** before interpreting the $\bar{X}$ chart!

#### VIII.B. Other Controls `[Apply]`
- Apply **Total Productive Maintenance (TPM)** (autonomous, preventive, predictive maintenance), **Visual Factory / Visual Controls** (andon lights, shadow boards, floor markings), **5S sustainment**, and **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) / Work Instructions**.

#### VIII.C. Maintain Controls `[Apply]`
- Establish **Control Plans** (process step, CTQ/characteristic, spec, measurement method, sample size/frequency, control method, **out-of-control action plan [OCAP] / reaction plan**).
- Re-verify MSA and process capability periodically; conduct layered process audits and update documentation.

#### VIII.D. Sustain Improvements `[Apply]`
- Transition ownership to the **Process Owner**, deliver ongoing role-based training, verify long-term financial gains with Finance, document **lessons learned**, and drive **horizontal replication / standardization** across other plants/business units.

### IX. Design for Six Sigma (DFSS) Framework and Methodologies (6 Questions)
*DMAIC Tag: **DFSS / Beyond DMAIC — New Product or Process Design (Domain IX)***

#### IX.A. Common DFSS Methodologies `[Understand]`
- Know when to transition from DMAIC (existing process hits an entitlement wall) to DFSS (designing a new product/service/process or radical redesign).
- Identify and describe:
  - **DMADV:** Define, Measure, Analyze, **Design, Verify/Validate**
  - **DMADOV:** Define, Measure, Analyze, **Design, Optimize, Verify/Validate** (also recognize IDOV).

#### IX.B. Design for X (DFX) `[Understand]`
- Describe design constraints and lifecycle considerations: **DFM/DFA** (Design for Manufacturability & Assembly), **DFC** (Cost), **DFT** (Testability), **DFMaint** (Maintainability/Serviceability), **DFR** (Reliability), and **DFE** (Environment/Sustainability).

#### IX.C. Robust Designs `[Evaluate]`
- Apply **Taguchi Robust Design** concepts:
  - **System Design -> Parameter Design -> Tolerance Design** (always optimize Parameter Design first to gain robustness at low cost before tightening expensive tolerances!).
  - **Control factors vs. Noise factors** (external/environmental, unit-to-unit, deterioration); inner/outer orthogonal arrays and **Signal-to-Noise ($S/N$) ratios** (Nominal-is-best, Smaller-is-better, Larger-is-better).
  - **Taguchi Quadratic Quality Loss Function:** $L(y) = k(y - T)^2$ (loss increases quadratically as output $y$ deviates from target $T$, contrasting with traditional goal-post step loss).
  - **Statistical vs. Worst-Case Tolerance Design:** $\sigma_{\text{assembly}} = \sqrt{\sigma_1^2 + \sigma_2^2 + \dots + \sigma_n^2}$ (root sum of squares!).
