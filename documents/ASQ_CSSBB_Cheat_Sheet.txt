1. ALL-IN-ONE HYPOTHESIS TEST PACKAGE (VI.B.3 & VI.B.4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. COMPARING MEANS (Continuous + NORMAL Data - Parametric)
- 1 Mean vs. Target (pop. sigma KNOWN)   : 1-Sample Z-test
- 1 Mean vs. Target (pop. sigma UNKNOWN) : 1-Sample t-test
- 2 Independent Means (Group A vs. B)    : 2-Sample t-test (Pooled = equal var; Welch = unequal var)
- 2 Paired Means (SAME items Before/After): Paired t-test
- 3+ Independent Means (4 Suppliers)     : One-Way ANOVA (Post-hoc pair check = Tukey / Fisher LSD)
- 3+ Means with 2 Factors (Shift + Mach.): Two-Way ANOVA (or Randomized Block ANOVA)

B. COMPARING MEDIANS / RANKS (Continuous SKEWED / Outliers or Ordinal - Non-Parametric)
- 1 Median vs. Target                    : 1-Sample Sign test  OR  1-Sample Wilcoxon Signed-Rank
- 2 Independent Medians (A vs. B, skewed): Mann-Whitney U test  (Non-normal twin of 2-Sample t)
- 2 Paired Medians (SAME items, skewed)  : Paired Wilcoxon Signed-Rank (Non-normal twin of Paired t)
- 3+ Independent Medians (3+ skewed)     : Kruskal-Wallis test  (Non-normal twin of One-Way ANOVA; or Mood's Median for wild outliers)
- 3+ Blocked / Related Medians (skewed)  : Friedman test        (Non-normal twin of Block ANOVA)

C. COMPARING VARIANCES / SPREAD (Consistency)
- 1 Variance vs. Target (Normal)         : 1-Variance Chi-Square test
- 2 Variances (Normal)                   : F-test
- 3+ Variances (Normal ONLY)             : Bartlett's test (very sensitive if non-normal!)
- 2 or 3+ Variances (Skewed / Non-Normal): Levene's test

D. COMPARING PROPORTIONS (% Pass/Fail, % Defective)
- 1 Proportion vs. Target %              : 1-Proportion Z-test
- 2 Proportions (Line 1 % vs. Line 2 %)  : 2-Proportion Z-test
- 3+ Proportions (Pass/Fail across 4+)   : Chi-Square Contingency Table test

E. COMPARING CATEGORY COUNTS / FREQUENCIES (Nominal Boxes - NEVER Averages!)
- 1 Category Set vs. Expected % Split    : Chi-Square Goodness-of-Fit test
- 2+ Categories in a Grid (Are X & Y tied?): Chi-Square Test of Independence (Expected cell >= 5)

F. CHECKING RANDOMNESS OVER TIME
- 1 Time-Ordered Sequence                : Runs test (checks clustering, trends, oscillation)

2. MEASUREMENT SYSTEMS ANALYSIS - MSA (V.C.2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ACCURACY = Average vs. True NIST Standard]
- Bias       : Average off at 1 single point (Fix: re-zero / calibrate)
- Linearity  : Bias changes across low, medium, high range (Fix: multi-point cal)
- Stability  : Accuracy drifts over TIME, e.g. Mon -> Wed -> Fri (Fix: X-bar/R chart)

[PRECISION = Spread / Scatter]
- Repeatability (EV)   : SAME operator, SAME part, SAME gage
- Reproducibility (AV) : DIFFERENT operators, SAME part, SAME gage

[ATTRIBUTE MSA & RESOLUTION]
- Kappa (Cohen/Fleiss) : Pass/Fail agreement beyond chance (>0.75 good, >0.90 excellent)
- Gage R&R %StudyVar   : <10% Acceptable | 10%-30% Marginal | >30% Unacceptable
- Resolution / ndc     : ndc >= 5; Gage must read 1/10th of tolerance (10-to-1 rule)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. NOIR MEASUREMENT SCALES (V.B.1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- N = Nominal  : Names/Labels only, no order (Shift A/B/C, Defect Type)
- O = Ordinal  : Ordered ranks, unequal gaps (1-to-5 Survey, 1st/2nd/3rd place)
- I = Interval : Equal gaps, NO true zero (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Calendar Year)
- R = Ratio    : Equal gaps WITH true zero (Time, Weight, Cost, Length, Kelvin)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PROBABILITY RULES & DISTRIBUTIONS (V.E)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Complement (NOT A)     : P(not A) = 1 - P(A)
- Addition (A OR B)      : P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B)
- Multiplication (A & B) : Independent: P(A)*P(B) | Dependent: P(A)*P(B|A)
- Permutations vs Combos : Order MATTERS = nPr | Order does NOT matter = nCr
- Binomial               : Fixed n trials, Pass/Fail (2 outcomes)
- Poisson                : Rare defects per unit, area, or time (scratches/sq m)
- Hypergeometric         : Sampling WITHOUT replacement from small finite lot
- Normal (Bell Curve)    : 68% (+/-1s), 95% (+/-2s), 99.7% (+/-3s)
- CLT (Sample Means)     : Spread = sigma / sqrt(n). To cut error in HALF -> 4x n!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. QUICK FORMULAS (V.A & V.F)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Little's Law : WIP = Throughput Rate * Lead Time  |  Lead Time = WIP / Rate
- Takt Time    : Available Production Time / Customer Demand
- RTY          : Step1 Yield * Step2 Yield * Step3 Yield ...
- Cp           : (USL - LSL) / (6 * short-term sigma)   [Potential, ignores center]
- Cpk          : Lower of (USL - Mean)/(3*s) or (Mean - LSL)/(3*s) [Actual]
- DPMO         : (Defects / (Units * Opportunities per unit)) * 1,000,000


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CONTROL CHARTS - DEFECTIVES vs. DEFECTS vs. VARIABLES (VIII.A)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. ATTRIBUTE: DEFECTIVES (Whole unit is Pass/Fail - Good or Bad)
- p-chart  : PERCENTAGE / FRACTION Defective (Sample size VARIES or Constant)
- np-chart : NUMBER / COUNT of Defectives    (Sample size MUST be CONSTANT)

B. ATTRIBUTE: DEFECTS (Count of flaws/blemishes - 1 unit can have multiple flaws)
- c-chart  : COUNT of Defects          (Inspection area/unit size MUST be CONSTANT)
- u-chart  : DEFECTS PER UNIT (rate)   (Inspection area/unit size VARIES or Constant)

C. CONTINUOUS / VARIABLES (Weight, Length, Time, Temperature)
- I-MR chart     : Subgroup size = 1 (individual readings)
- X-bar & R chart: Subgroup size = 2 to 9
- X-bar & s chart: Subgroup size = 10 or more

D. CONTROL CHART SIGNALS
- Common-Cause (In Control / Stable): Random bounce inside control limits
- Special-Cause (Out of Control)    : 1 point outside limits, OR 8-9 points on one side of centerline, OR 6+ points trending steadily up/down

7. CORRELATION & LINEAR REGRESSION (VI.A.1 & VI.A.2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Correlation (r)    : -1.0 to +1.0 (no units). +1 = upward line, -1 = downward line, 0 = no linear link.
  - Traps            : Correlation != causation; U-curve can give r = 0 (plot Scatter Diagram first!).
  - Significance t   : t = r * sqrt( (n - 2) / (1 - r^2) ), df = n - 2
- R-squared          : (r)^2 -> % of variation in Y explained by X (always 0% to 100%).
- Simple Regression  : Predicted Y = b0 + (b1 * X)
  - b1 (Slope)       : Change in Y when X goes up by 1 unit [b1 = r * (sy / sx)]
  - b0 (Intercept)   : Predicted Y when X = 0 [b0 = Y_bar - b1 * X_bar]
  - Extrapolation    : NEVER predict outside the tested X data range.
- Multiple Regression:
  - Adjusted R-sq    : Use instead of R-sq when adding X's (penalizes useless X's).
  - VIF              : Checks Multicollinearity (duplicate X's). VIF = 1 ideal; VIF > 5 or > 10 = bad.
- 4 Residual Checks  : 1) Normal, 2) Constant Variance (no funnel/cone), 3) Independent over time (Durbin-Watson ~ 2.0), 4) No high-leverage outliers.
- CI vs. PI          : Confidence Interval (narrower) = average Y; Prediction Interval (wider) = 1 single future Y.
- Logistic Regression: Use when Y is Binary (Pass/Fail, Yes/No, 0/1).

8. NINE QUICK 2-WORD TRAPS TO NEVER SWAP (FROM COPILOT SET 4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Alpha (Type I) vs. Power : Alpha = Type I Error (False Alarm); Beta = Type II Error (Miss); Power = 1 - Beta.
- Outlier Resistance       : MEDIAN & IQR resist outliers (Mean, SD, and Range get pulled by outliers!).
- VOC vs. CTQ              : VOC = raw customer words/feedback; CTQ = measurable requirement translated from VOC.
- c-chart vs. np-chart     : c-chart = count of DEFECTS (flaws, constant area); np-chart = count of DEFECTIVES (bad units, constant n).
- X-bar vs. R chart        : X-bar chart catches MEAN shifts; R chart catches SPREAD / VARIABILITY shifts.
- Control vs. Spec Limits  : Control Limits (UCL/LCL) = Voice of PROCESS (VOP); Spec Limits (USL/LSL) = Voice of CUSTOMER (VOC).
- Cp vs. Cpk               : Cp = spread only (ignores centering); Cpk = spread AND centering ("k" = centering).
- H0 vs. Ha                : Null (H0) = NO difference / status quo ("0" = zero effect); Alternative (Ha) = desired improvement / difference.
- Simple Random vs. Strat. : Simple Random = EVERY item has equal chance; Stratified = split into groups first, then sample each group.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. QUALITY, SIX SIGMA & LEAN PIONEERS (10 NAMES)
- Walter Shewhart   : Father of SPC -> invented CONTROL CHARTS, Common vs. Special Cause, PDSA cycle.
- W. Edwards Deming : PDCA cycle, 14 Points for Management -> 85%-94% of defects come from the SYSTEM, not workers.
- Joseph Juran      : QUALITY TRILOGY (Planning, Control, Improvement), "Fitness for Use", Pareto Vital Few vs. Trivial Many.
- Philip Crosby     : "Quality is Free", "ZERO DEFECTS" (do it right the first time), Conformance to Requirements.
- Kaoru Ishikawa    : Invented FISHBONE (Cause-and-Effect) diagram, 7 Basic Quality Tools, Quality Circles.
- Armand Feigenbaum : TOTAL QUALITY CONTROL (TQC) -> quality is everyone's job across all departments ("Hidden Plant").
- Genichi Taguchi   : ROBUST DESIGN (insensitive to noise) + QUALITY LOSS FUNCTION (any drift from exact target costs money).
- Taiichi Ohno      : Father of TOYOTA PRODUCTION SYSTEM (TPS), Just-In-Time (JIT) pull, 7 Lean Wastes (Muda).
- Shigeo Shingo     : Invented POKA-YOKE (mistake-proofing) and SMED (Single-Minute Exchange of Die / quick setups).
- Bill Smith        : Coined SIX SIGMA at Motorola (1986, 3.4 DPMO); later scaled company-wide by Jack Welch at GE.

B. CHANGE MANAGEMENT LEADERS (3 MODELS)
- Kurt Lewin (3 Stages)     : UNFREEZE (show why old way is broken) -> CHANGE (train/adopt new way) -> REFREEZE (lock into SOPs) + Force Field Analysis.
- John Kotter (8 Steps)     : Step 1 = Create Urgency -> Step 2 = Guiding Coalition -> Step 6 = Short-Term Wins -> Step 8 = Anchor in Culture.
- William Bridges (3 Phases): Psychological Transition -> 1) Ending/Letting Go -> 2) Neutral Zone (in-between confusion) -> 3) New Beginning.
