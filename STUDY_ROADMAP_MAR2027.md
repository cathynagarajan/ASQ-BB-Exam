- [ ] **Week 2 — Org Planning & Process Measures (Domains I & II: 24 Qs):** Lean + Six Sigma foundations, Maturity Models, SWOT, Benchmarking, Balanced Scorecard, KPIs/KBIs/OKRs, COQ (PAF model), Financial calculations (ROI, NPV, IRR, Payback, CBA).

### Stage 2: Measure Phase Deep Dive (Weeks 4–7 | Mid-Dec 2026 – Early Jan 2027)
*Covers **25 / 150 questions (16.7%)** — Single biggest domain on the ASQ exam!*
- [ ] **Week 4 — Process Characteristics & Data Collection (V.A–V.B):** Process maps, VSM, Takt time, Cycle time, Little's Law, Hidden Factory, FTY vs. RTY, NOIR scales, Sampling methods.
- [ ] **Week 5 — Measurement Systems Analysis / MSA (V.C):** Bias, Linearity, Stability, Gage R&R ($\bar{X}\text{-}R$ & ANOVA), %StudyVar vs. P/T vs. %Contribution, $ndc$, Attribute Agreement / Kappa.
- [ ] **Week 6 — Basic Statistics & Probability (V.D–V.E):** Descriptive stats, CLT, Boxplots/Multi-Vari/Normal plots, Enumerative vs. Analytic, Probability rules, Binomial, Poisson, Hypergeometric, Normal, $t$, $\chi^2$, $F$.

- [ ] **Week 8 — RCA, Waste & Risk/FMEA (VI.C–VI.D):** Fishbone, 5 Whys, Fault Tree Analysis (AND/OR gates), Gap & Waste analysis (TIMWOODS), DFMEA/PFMEA ($S, O, D$, RPN, Action Priority), Risk Management.
- [ ] **Week 9 — Parametric Hypothesis Testing & CIs (VI.B.1–VI.B.3):** $\alpha, \beta$, Power, $p$-values, Sample Size, Confidence/Prediction/Tolerance intervals, $Z$, $t$ (1-sample, 2-sample, paired), $F$/Bartlett/Levene, Proportions, 1-way/2-way ANOVA, $\chi^2$ contingency tables.
